<p>Matewave is a free chess app that works fully offline. Play the computer, solve puzzles at your level, learn openings step by step, or play a friend sitting next to you. No account, no ads, no online matchmaking — just chess.</p>

<p><b>♟️ Play against the AI</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Four levels: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert.</li>
<li>Good for learning the game or a quick match on the go.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>🧩 Puzzles for your rating</b></p>
<ul>
<li>1,200+ puzzles from about 400 to 2800, picked to match your level.</li>
<li>Mate in 1, 2 and 3, forks, pins, skewers, discovered attacks, deflection, sacrifices, hanging pieces and zugzwang.</li>
<li>Endgame practice: rook, pawn, bishop and knight endings.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>📖 Opening trainer</b></p>
<ul>
<li>24 courses: 10 for White and 14 for Black.</li>
<li>White: Italian, Ruy Lopez, Scotch, Vienna Gambit, King's Gambit, Queen's Gambit (Declined and Accepted), London System, English and Catalan.</li>
<li>Black: Sicilian Najdorf and Dragon, French, Caro-Kann, Scandinavian, Pirc, Petrov, Slav, King's Indian, Nimzo-Indian, Grünfeld, Dutch Leningrad and Tarrasch.</li>
<li>One new opening at a time, and spaced repetition brings each line back until it sticks. Earn up to three stars per opening.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>📶 Play with friends nearby</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Bluetooth or the same Wi-Fi, with automatic discovery of nearby players.</li>
<li>Up to 20 friends on the same Wi-Fi can watch a live game.</li>
<li>Share the app with a friend by QR code.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>🎷 And a little more</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Optional jazz radio while you play.</li>
<li>Dark design with five piece sets to choose from.</li>
<li>English, Spanish, Lithuanian, Russian, German, Japanese and Chinese.</li>
<li>Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).</li>
</ul>
